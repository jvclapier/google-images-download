# Chicken Ranch Pasta

**From the Kitchen of:** Linda Clapier
**Doc tab:** Main Dishes › Chicken Ranch Pasta
**Transcribed:** 2026-08-21 from a handwritten Stylecraft recipe card (69-930)

## Ingredients

- 3–4 pkgs. bow tie pasta
- 2 squares butter
- 4 pkgs. dry ranch dressing mix
- 2 good season italian mix
- Milk to taste, 4 c. (up to 8 c.)
- Chicken breasts
- Bacon

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta.
2. Melt the butter. Add the dry ranch dressing mix and the good season italian mix,
   then wisk together with milk to taste.
3. Grill or broil chicken breasts — cut to bite size pieces.
4. Bacon — brown & crumble.
5. Toss together all ingredients — serve.
